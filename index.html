The Michigan kitchen-table card game. No account, no ads, no analytics, no tracking.

Privacy Support

Privacy policy

Effective October 26, 2026.

The short version

Kitchen Table: Five Hundred has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Playing against the bots, the tutorial, and passing the phone around the table all stay on your device and send nothing anywhere.

What is sent when you play online

An online table is joined with a room code. While you are at an online table, the name you type, your seat, a random player number the app made on your device, and the game's bids, cards and moves are sent to the other players in that room. They are relayed in real time through a hosted messaging service, Supabase. The app does not save games, players, names or moves in a database, and nothing is kept after the messages are delivered. Supabase may process technical data such as your IP address to make the connection, under its own privacy policy.

Anyone who knows a room code can try to join that room, so share codes only with the people you want at your table. The name you enter is visible to everyone at that table. There is no chat.

What stays on your device

The app remembers your settings (ruleset, sound, haptics, table colour), your play statistics, whether Full Table is unlocked, and, for an online table, your seat and player number so that a reload can put you back in your seat. This is stored only on the device and is deleted when you delete the app.

Purchases

Full Table is a one-time purchase processed by Apple. The app only asks the App Store whether Full Table is unlocked on your Apple account and stores that yes-or-no on the device. We never receive your payment details or keep purchase records.

What we do not collect

We do not collect contacts, location, photos, microphone or camera data, the advertising identifier, or payment details. We do not sell or share personal information, and nothing is used for advertising or tracking.

Third parties

The only outside services are Apple (App Store and purchases) and Supabase (relaying online table messages). Neither receives data from the app for advertising, and Supabase only relays messages for the room you are in.

Keeping and deleting your data

We hold no data about you on any server, so there is nothing to request or delete from us. Leaving an online table stops anything further being sent. Deleting the app removes everything it stored on your device. Settings > Reset stats clears your statistics at any time.

Children

The game has no accounts and no chat and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If a future version changes what the app sends or stores, for example by adding accounts, this page will be updated before that version is released, with a new effective date.

How do I play with friends?

One person opens Live Game and hosts a table (part of Full Table). Everyone else opens Live Game and joins with the room code. Empty seats are filled by bots, so two, three or four people can play.

Someone dropped out of an online game

A bot takes their seat and keeps playing. If they come back within the rejoin window, using the same phone, they get their seat back. If the host leaves, the table ends.

I bought Full Table on another device

Open Settings > Full Table and tap Restore Purchases while signed in to the same Apple account.

Which rules does it use?

Michigan kitchen-table 500: 45-card deck with the joker, a five-card kitty, six is a real bid, Nello is 250 and Double Nello is 500. First team to 500 wins; a team at −500 loses. Settings > Open full rules has the details.
